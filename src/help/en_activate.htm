Activation and trial

Trial edition

Without a product key the program runs as a trial limited to 30 days or 30 conversations, whichever comes first. Trial state is kept in HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Axiom\NeuroBench2003 and mirrored to Application Data\9xHarness\NEBENCH.LIC.

Do not edit those values by hand: the program notices and marks the licence as corrupted.

Product keys (CDK)

A key is 5 groups of 4 characters separated by hyphens:

2K7M-4XQ9-BT3P-DE8N-VW6C

The alphabet omits 0, 1, I and O to avoid misreading. Case does not matter and the hyphens are optional.

Activating

  1. Choose Tools - Activate Product.
  2. Type the key.
  3. Press Activate. The status bar then shows "Licensed (Standard / Professional / Enterprise)".

If the key is rejected

  • Check for missing or extra characters (20 characters, hyphens excluded);
  • Make sure it is a 9xHarness key and not one for another product;
  • Otherwise send the key and your invoice to your dealer.
Time-limited keys: some keys carry a validity period (for example 365 days). It counts from the day the program first ran; when it expires the program reports "trial expired" and needs a new key.
